## About

Proof of Concept is a publication for the Design GM: designers, builders, and founders learning to lead, build, and operate like owners.

A Design GM is a designer who operates like a general manager: leading teams, building products, and taking responsibility for business outcomes. It’s a way of working, whether or not GM is in your title.

Every Sunday, David Hoang writes about the judgment, craft, and experiments behind that work. Essays explore design leadership, building products, entrepreneurship, and the business of design, with room for taste, ecosystems, and agent experience.

Lead the work, own the bets, run the experiments. Subscribe free for one issue every Sunday.

## Short author bio

David Hoang is a designer, writer, and investor. He leads design for Rovo & AI and Ecosystem at Atlassian, with previous roles at Replit, Webflow, and One Medical. He writes Proof of Concept, Sunday essays for Design GMs on leadership, building products, and the business of design.

## Editorial decisions

The pasted “13,000+” subscriber count and proposed member benefits, including full-archive access and a quarterly brief, were unverified proposals. Those claims are excluded from this copy and the website changes. The free Sunday issue is the subscription promise used here.

## Discovery and measurement

Use a clear Design GM definition, consistent publication and author names, useful internal reading links, and metadata that matches visible copy. Google says its existing SEO fundamentals also apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode; extra AI-specific markup is unnecessary, and inclusion or ranking is not guaranteed. [Google Search Central: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

After deployment, compare search impressions and clicks, known AI referrals, and newsletter submit attempts against the prior period. Google includes AI-feature traffic in Search Console’s overall Web reporting. Confirm completed subscriptions in Substack: this site can observe the signup attempt, not the outcome after the form leaves its origin.
